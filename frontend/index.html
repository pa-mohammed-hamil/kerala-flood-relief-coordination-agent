KERALA FLOOD RELIEF COORDINATION AGENT

Intelligent Flood Monitoring, Emergency Coordination & Human-Supervised AI

A proposed multi-agent disaster management platform that brings together flood forecasting, weather and river monitoring, emergency incident management, rescue planning, evacuation coordination, shelter intelligence, medical response, resource allocation and AI-assisted decision support.

Designed around Python, FastAPI, machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, advanced RAG, multi-agent orchestration and human approval. Current standalone frontend demonstrations use synthetic data and local interactions.

Safety-by-Design
AI outputs should be treated as decision support, never as independent authorization to dispatch responders, direct evacuations, issue official alerts or operate infrastructure.
