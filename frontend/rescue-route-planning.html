⚠ Life-Safety Warning.
This application does not know whether any real road, bridge, water crossing, evacuation route or destination is safe. It uses an invented graph with fictional distances, closures and hazard scores. Never use its outputs for actual rescue or evacuation decisions. Production routing requires authoritative GIS networks, verified closures, live flood observations, vehicle constraints, communication with authorized responders and human command approval. For emergencies in India, call 112.
